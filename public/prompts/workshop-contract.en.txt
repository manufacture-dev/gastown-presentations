During this workshop, work incrementally and always wait for my explicit request before creating or dispatching work.

Delivery guardrails:
- the delivery branch is the source of truth;
- a bead is complete only after merge and verification on that branch;
- create atomic beads with one owner, dependencies, and acceptance criteria;
- merge foundations before dependent work;
- give shared integration files to a single owner;
- if a conflict occurs, preserve the branch and report the blocker instead of claiming completion.

Keep replies concise and make the real delivery state observable.
